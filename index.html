What We Actually Do

Cross-Country Hare-Scramble Racing

MWSS races are cross-country hare-scramble style events — wheel-to-wheel racing on a circuit, with laps that run 5 to 10 miles and change character every few hundred yards. One minute you're on a dirt track or trail, the next you're crossing an open field, threading through tight trees, picking a line through rocks, plowing through a water crossing, or fighting for traction on a steep climb or descent.

It's less about clean qualifying laps and more about surviving whatever the terrain throws at you — which, in our experience, is usually something we didn't expect.
